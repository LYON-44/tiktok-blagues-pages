Privacy Policy

TikTok-Blagues · Effective 23 August 2026

This policy explains how TikTok-Blagues handles information when the TikTok account owner authorizes the application.

Information processed

Depending on permissions approved by the user, the application may process basic TikTok account information such as open ID, display name and avatar; public video identifiers and metadata; performance data such as view, like, comment and share counts; and technical authorization tokens required to access TikTok APIs.

How information is used

Information is used only to identify the authorized account, create or deliver comedy-content drafts, list public videos, analyze their performance, maintain the content queue, and diagnose technical failures.

Storage and security

Authorization credentials are stored in restricted server-side secrets and are not intentionally exposed in public logs or client-side pages. Reasonable technical measures limit access to the application operator.

Sharing and sale

Personal information is not sold or shared for advertising. Data is disclosed only to service providers needed to operate the application, including TikTok, GitHub-hosted automation, and content-generation services, or when required by law.

Retention

Authorization tokens are retained while the connection is active or until replaced or revoked. Video identifiers, metrics, and operational records are retained only as reasonably needed to operate and improve the application.

User choices

The user may decline permissions or revoke TikTok access at any time. To request access, correction, or deletion of application-held data, contact the operator.

Children

The application is not directed to children and is intended only for an authorized TikTok account owner who meets TikTok's age requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated when application practices or legal requirements change.

Contact

Privacy requests may be submitted through the LYON-44 GitHub profile.
